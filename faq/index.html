If you would like to receive your Degree Certificate by post, please follow the steps below:

  1. Log in to the GLA University Portal:
    https://glauniversity.in:8085/Main/Index
  2. Navigate to the "Service Request" section under the "OTHER" tab on the left side of the screen.
  3. Fill in the required details and mention your Name and Roll Number in the remarks section.
  4. Submit the request and make the required payment:
    • Degree Certificate (Postal Charges): ₹500
    • Migration Certificate: ₹400
  5. After making the payment, download the payment receipt and send it via email along with the following details:
    • Name
    • Email ID
    • Complete and accurate mailing address
    • Mobile Number
    • Recent Aadhaar Card copy (for verification)

Important:

  • The postal delivery service is available only for addresses within India.
  • Ensure that the mailing address provided is complete and accurate to avoid delivery issues.
