Cdoe Alumni Meet

Showcasing the vibrant journey of GLA Online Alumni through memorable experiences.

  1. Home
  2. Engage Learn Celebrate
  3. Cdoe Alumni Meet

The *GLA CDOE Alumni Association* hosted a memorable Alumni Meet on *22 August*, bringing together esteemed alumni for an enriching celebration of their enduring connection with GLA University. The event provided a warm and engaging platform for alumni to reconnect with their alma mater, revisit cherished memories and strengthen the bonds that continue beyond graduation.

The gathering witnessed meaningful interactions and inspiring conversations as alumni shared their professional journeys, experiences and valuable insights.

Their stories served as a source of motivation for the CDOE community and highlighted the diverse paths that GLAdiators have taken in their careers.

The presence of our alumni made the occasion truly special, reflecting the strong sense of belonging and camaraderie that defines the GLA family. The meet also reinforced the Association’s commitment to fostering *lifelong engagement, networking, mentorship and meaningful alumni–university connections*.

The CDOE Alumni Meet was a celebration of shared memories, achievements and the enduring spirit of the GLA community.

The CDOE Alumni Meet 2026 continued with a warm networking lunch at Appetito, bringing alumni together to reconnect, share experiences, and strengthen professional bonds.

Anurag Chowdhary (MBA, Batch of 2024), Project Manager at Indian Oil Corporation, was felicitated during the CDOE Alumni Meet 2026 by Dr. Diwakar Bhardwaj (PVC) and Dr. Suresh Chandra Joshi (Director, CDOE), celebrating his professional journey and achievements.

Umesh Pratap Singh (MBA, Batch of 2024), Sr. Associate at Zomato Hyperpure Pvt. Ltd., was felicitated at the CDOE Alumni Meet 2026, celebrating his professional growth, achievements, and the spirit of staying connected with GLA.

A memorable post-discussion group photograph bringing together the dignitaries, CDOE and Alumni Association team, and CDOE alumni—celebrating shared experiences, meaningful conversations, and the GLA bond.

A productive interaction bringing together the dignitaries, CDOE and Alumni Association team, and CDOE alumni, fostering meaningful dialogue, shared insights, and stronger connections.

A relaxed high-tea gathering with Mrs. Deepti Kapur (AGM, Alumni Association) and CDOE alumni, creating a warm space for light conversations, shared memories, and meaningful connections.
