Frequently Asked Questions About Online B.com

Got Questions? We've Got Answers!

General

Yes. The Online B.Com. (Hons.) program at GLA Online University is UGC-entitled, which ensures that the degree is valid for employment, higher education and government examinations.

Candidates must have passed 10 + 2 (or equivalent) from a recognised board, in any stream.

The curriculum includes core areas such as Financial Accounting, Cost Accounting, Corporate Accounting, Taxation (Income Tax, GST), Auditing, Business Law, Economics, Business Statistics and Digital Finance tools. These are designed to build analytical, decision-making and financial‐management skills.

The program offers fully online delivery with recorded lectures, interactive content and self-paced scheduling. Learners can balance education alongside personal or professional commitments, studying from anywhere at their convenience.

GLA Online offers 360° career support, including resume building, mock interviews, mentorship sessions and access to placement opportunities through its industry and alumni network.

Graduates may work as Accountants, Tax Consultants, Auditors, Financial Analysts, Corporate Finance Executives, or in banking and allied financial services. The program also provides a strong base for further studies such as CA, CMA, MBA (Finance), or CPA.
