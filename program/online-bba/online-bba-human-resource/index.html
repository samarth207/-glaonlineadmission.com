FAQs

Got Questions? We've Got Answers!

General

Yes. The Online BBA at GLA Online University is “UGC‐approved” / government recognised, ensuring that the degree is valid for employment, further studies and government examinations.

Candidates must have passed the 10 + 2 (or equivalent) from a recognised board in any stream to be eligible for the Online BBA.

The program offers a four specializations such as Marketing Management, Finance Management, Human Resource Management, and Business Logistic & Supply Chain Management

The Online BBA provides a fully online mode of delivery: self-paced access to recorded lectures, interactive digital modules and flexible scheduling – thereby allowing learners to balance education with personal and/or professional commitments.

The Online division of GLA Online University offers dedicated career-support services including resume building, mock interviews, mentorship and access to placement opportunities with their industry partners.

The Online BBA in Human Resource Management offers a variety of career opportunities by developing essential skills in talent acquisition, employee relations, training and development, performance management, and HR operations. Common entry-level roles include HR Executive, Recruitment Coordinator, Talent Acquisition Associate, HR Operations Assistant, Payroll Executive, and Training & Development Assistant. As professionals gain experience, they can move into roles such as HR Manager, Talent Manager, Learning & Development Specialist, Employee Relations Manager, or HR Business Partner. The specialization prepares learners for roles in corporate organizations, consulting firms, staffing agencies, and growing startups, helping them contribute effectively to workforce management and organizational development.
