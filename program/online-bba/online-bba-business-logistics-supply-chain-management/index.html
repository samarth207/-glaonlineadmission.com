OVERVIEW

BBA with specialization in Business Logistics & Supply Chain Management by GLA Online is designed for learners who aspire to master the art of movement - of products, processes, and progress. From understanding global trade networks to optimizing logistics operations, this program combines analytical thinking with real-world business applications.

Learners gain insights into procurement, inventory management, warehouse control, and supply chain analytics - making them future-ready professionals who can navigate the complexities of modern commerce with confidence and skill.

General

Yes. The Online BBA at GLA Online University is “UGC‐approved” / government recognised, ensuring that the degree is valid for employment, further studies and government examinations.

Candidates must have passed the 10 + 2 (or equivalent) from a recognised board in any stream to be eligible for the Online BBA.

The program offers a four specializations such as Marketing Management, Finance Management, Human Resource Management, and Business Logistic & Supply Chain Management

The Online BBA provides a fully online mode of delivery: self-paced access to recorded lectures, interactive digital modules and flexible scheduling – thereby allowing learners to balance education with personal and/or professional commitments.

The Online division of GLA Online University offers dedicated career-support services including resume building, mock interviews, mentorship and access to placement opportunities with their industry partners.

The Online BBA in Business Logistics & Supply Chain Management opens career opportunities across manufacturing, e-commerce, retail, transportation, warehousing, and logistics service companies by building strong foundations in procurement, inventory control, operations, and distribution management. Typical entry-level roles include Logistics Coordinator, Supply Chain Executive, Inventory Analyst, Procurement Assistant, Warehouse Operations Executive, and Transportation Planner. With experience, professionals can progress into positions such as Supply Chain Manager, Operations Manager, Procurement Manager, Logistics Manager, or Distribution Manager. The specialization equips learners with practical skills to streamline operations, reduce costs, and optimize end-to-end supply chain processes in both traditional and rapidly growing digital commerce environments.
