Mentor-Mentee Open House Session – Tax Planning vs Tax Evasion: Smart Saving or Financial Crime?

Speaker: Ms. Rakhi Sharma, Assistant Professor, CDOE, GLA University

26.Jun.26

The session focused on helping learners understand the difference between legitimate tax planning and illegal tax evasion. Participants learned how effective tax planning can help individuals legally reduce their tax liability through appropriate financial decisions related to salary, investments, insurance, and personal expenses. The session also highlighted common practices associated with tax evasion and explained their potential legal and financial consequences. The session aimed to promote responsible financial decision-making and awareness of tax compliance.
